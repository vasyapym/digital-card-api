# Tomatoes

Tomatoes are a warm-season crop. See also [[Plants]] and [[Сад/Томаты|the Russian note]]. #seedlings

## Varieties

### Cherry

Small and sweet. Grown alongside [[Земляника#Уход|strawberries]].

### Beefsteak

Large fruit for slicing; sow indoors eight weeks before the last frost.
